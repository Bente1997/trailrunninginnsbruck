01 / The beginning

A way to feel at home.

DNA Trails started in May 2023 with a small group of Dutch and German expats. We knew how much sport had helped us connect after moving to Innsbruck. The city had athletes everywhere, but accessible trail running communities were harder to find.

So we started meeting on Thursdays. No club fees, no complicated sign-ups. Just a run, and a way to meet like-minded people.

02 / Word got around

A few runners became a community.

After we started sharing our runs on Instagram in summer 2023, more and more people joined. Sometimes over 50 runners would turn up.

The name DNA Trails reflects what we believe: the urge to run in the mountains lives in our DNA, and that DNA connects us all.

03 / Still the same idea

Keep it open. Keep it human.

Our community is run by volunteers. We still believe a good run starts with a warm welcome, and that the best pace is the one that lets everyone belong.
